### Stop-the-Line Criteria

- **Contract breach** – A core API contract is violated (e.g., missing fields in responses or changed endpoint behavior) despite the refactor:contentReference[oaicite:28]{index=28}.
- **Data integrity issue** – Any evidence of graph corruption or missing expected relationships (e.g., Documents not linked to Chunks) after refactor deployment.
- **Security regression** – Detection of sensitive information (API keys, secrets) appearing in logs or outputs, indicating scrubbing failures:contentReference[oaicite:29]{index=29}.
- **Test suite failure** – Smoke tests or critical integration tests do not pass after a refactor phase, indicating a regression in functionality:contentReference[oaicite:30]{index=30}.
- **Unstable operations** – The system exhibits crashes, severe performance degradation, or inability to ingest/search data that previously worked (sign of major regression).
